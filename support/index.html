Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about ScoreSensei

What devices are supported?

ScoreSensei is available on iOS devices running iOS 17 or later. Download it from the App Store. Android support is planned for the future.

Is ScoreSensei free?

ScoreSensei is available on the App Store with a free trial. You can analyze your first round and form video at no cost to experience the full feature set. After that, a subscription unlocks unlimited access to all features including form analysis, round tracking, advanced stats, and more.

How does form analysis work?

Record your throw from a side angle using your phone. You'll see skeletal tracking overlaid on your video, side-by-side comparison with optimal pro form, timing breakdowns at key checkpoints, and personalized coaching tips to improve your technique.

How do I track my rounds?

Use hands-free voice scoring while you play—just speak your score after each hole. ScoreSensei automatically tracks advanced stats like fairway hit percentage, circle 1 putting, scramble rate, and more. After your round, you'll get detailed insights, visual breakdowns, and shareable round stories.

Can I delete my account and data?

Yes. Contact us at scoresenseifeedback@gmail.com to request account deletion, and we'll permanently remove all your data within 30 days.

How do I report bugs or suggest features?

We'd love to hear from you! Email us at scoresenseifeedback@gmail.com with any feedback, bug reports, or feature requests. Your input helps make ScoreSensei better for everyone.
